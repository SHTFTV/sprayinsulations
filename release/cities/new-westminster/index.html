Insulation projects in New Westminster

Prepare a useful brief for an attic, crawl space, basement or renovation in New Westminster. Start with the condition of the building and the work you need assessed.

Make the first assessment useful

For your New Westminster enquiry, describe where the discomfort or damage occurs, when you first noticed it, and whether work has already opened the walls or ceiling. Include accessible photos and rough dimensions. A contractor can then explain which conditions need investigation before selecting insulation.

Moisture, access, existing materials and ventilation belong in the same conversation as the insulation product. Request an assessment of the actual building assembly, not a thickness chosen from a generic price list.

Local preparation for New Westminster

In New Westminster, check heritage status before deciding how to reach an existing wall or ceiling. The City identifies several forms of heritage protection and directs owners in Queen’s Park to its conservation-area guidance. The relevant question is whether your proposed alteration affects protected features, not simply whether the house looks old.

Confirm these details

  • Is the property protected or within the Queen’s Park Heritage Conservation Area?
  • Which finishes must remain, and how will the work area be accessed?
  • Has the proposed alteration been discussed with the City before installation is scheduled?

Official local guidance: Heritage Alteration Permits. Checked September 28, 2026. Confirm the route for your exact scope with the municipality.

Match the guide to the part of your building

  • Attic: access, existing condition and roof-space planning
  • Crawl space: moisture history, low clearance and services
  • Basement: existing finishes and assessment scope
  • Spray foam: product selection and project-specific questions

What should a written proposal explain?

Ask for the treated area, preparation, product identification, exclusions and responsibilities. Confirm who handles access, occupant coordination, any separate repairs, cleanup and the completion record. If the conditions change after the work starts, agree how a revised scope is approved.
