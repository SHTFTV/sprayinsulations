Insulation projects in Burnaby

Prepare a useful brief for an attic, crawl space, basement or renovation in Burnaby. Start with the condition of the building and the work you need assessed.

Make the first assessment useful

For your Burnaby enquiry, describe where the discomfort or damage occurs, when you first noticed it, and whether work has already opened the walls or ceiling. Include accessible photos and rough dimensions. A contractor can then explain which conditions need investigation before selecting insulation.

Moisture, access, existing materials and ventilation belong in the same conversation as the insulation product. Request an assessment of the actual building assembly, not a thickness chosen from a generic price list.

Local preparation for Burnaby

Burnaby distinguishes alterations that do not add floor area from additions and restoration work. That is useful when preparing an insulation enquiry: opening an existing ceiling, extending a room and repairing a damaged assembly may follow different application routes. Identify which type of project you have before committing to an installation date.

Confirm these details

  • Is this an alteration, an addition or restoration of an existing Burnaby building?
  • Does the proposed treatment affect a shared wall or ceiling requiring strata coordination?
  • Which approved drawing shows the treatment boundary and inspection access?

Official local guidance: Home Improvement Permits. Checked September 28, 2026. Confirm the route for your exact scope with the municipality.

Match the guide to the part of your building

  • Attic: access, existing condition and roof-space planning
  • Crawl space: moisture history, low clearance and services
  • Basement: existing finishes and assessment scope
  • Spray foam: product selection and project-specific questions

What should a written proposal explain?

Ask for the treated area, preparation, product identification, exclusions and responsibilities. Confirm who handles access, occupant coordination, any separate repairs, cleanup and the completion record. If the conditions change after the work starts, agree how a revised scope is approved.
