LOWER MAINLAND · LOCAL PROJECT PLANNING

Insulation projects in Delta

Prepare a useful brief for an attic, crawl space, basement or renovation in Delta. Start with the condition of the building and the work you need assessed.

Make the first assessment useful

For your Delta enquiry, describe where the discomfort or damage occurs, when you first noticed it, and whether work has already opened the walls or ceiling. Include accessible photos and rough dimensions. A contractor can then explain which conditions need investigation before selecting insulation.

Moisture, access, existing materials and ventilation belong in the same conversation as the insulation product. Request an assessment of the actual building assembly, not a thickness chosen from a generic price list.

Local preparation for Delta

Delta’s building guidance directs owners to different submission requirements for residential, non-residential and secondary-suite projects. When insulation is included in a basement finish, suite alteration or commercial renovation, describe that wider project to the Application Centre. Confirm the requirements for the actual scope instead of treating every insulation job as the same kind of repair.

Confirm these details

  • Is the work part of a dwelling renovation, secondary suite or non-residential project?
  • Has Delta’s Application Centre confirmed the submission requirements for this scope?
  • Which inspection must take place before the treated area is enclosed?

Official local guidance: Building & Renovating. Checked September 28, 2026. Confirm the route for your exact scope with the municipality.

Match the guide to the part of your building

  • Attic: access, existing condition and roof-space planning
  • Crawl space: moisture history, low clearance and services
  • Basement: existing finishes and assessment scope
  • Spray foam: product selection and project-specific questions

What should a written proposal explain?

Ask for the treated area, preparation, product identification, exclusions and responsibilities. Confirm who handles access, occupant coordination, any separate repairs, cleanup and the completion record. If the conditions change after the work starts, agree how a revised scope is approved.
