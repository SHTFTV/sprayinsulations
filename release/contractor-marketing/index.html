FOR CONTRACTORS & CONSTRUCTION BUSINESSES

Directory and SEO marketing, with a clear scope.

Build a useful business presence around what you actually do, where you work and the evidence you can share. Construction marketing enquiries are handled through BuildersHaus, part of the IAM network.

Start with a credible contractor profile

A useful profile tells a property owner your business name, actual services, service area and preferred enquiry route. Include a concise description, three relevant service keywords and examples you have permission to publish. Identify who owns the business information and who will keep it current.

Claims about qualifications, insurance, completed projects or customer reviews need supporting evidence before they are presented as verified. A profile enquiry does not automatically create a public listing or imply endorsement. Directory onboarding and marketplace gates remain closed while the system is being prepared.

Local content that answers real questions

Discuss pages that explain a service in the context of the places you genuinely serve. Useful content can cover assessment preparation, access arrangements, scope comparisons and project handover. It should explain the business’s actual capabilities rather than suggest an office or project history in every city.

Bring original project photographs with publication permission, approved descriptions and source documents. Distinguish your own work from stock or illustrative imagery. BuildersHaus can discuss the content and directory work appropriate to your business; availability and deliverables are agreed before work begins.

Define the SEO work before buying it

An initial review can identify unclear navigation, missing service information, inconsistent contact details or pages that search engines cannot access. A proposed scope should state which pages and technical checks are included, who supplies evidence, who approves copy and what completion records you receive.

Search Console ownership, sitemap acceptance, successful fetch, discovered URLs and page indexing are different milestones. Ask for reporting that distinguishes them. Rankings, publication timing, traffic, enquiries and sales are not guaranteed by a listing or an SEO engagement.

Discuss city or directory partnerships

Explain your service area, capacity and the type of customer you want to reach. Any placement, content partnership, commercial disclosure and ongoing maintenance responsibilities require a separate agreement. Sending a message does not reserve a city, grant exclusivity or activate a paid membership.

Payment and marketplace gates remain closed. There is no checkout on this page. Request the current proposed scope and terms directly rather than assuming a package is active.

Send your public website, business name, service area, three service keywords and the main issue you want to address. Do not send passwords, customer records or payment details in an initial enquiry.
