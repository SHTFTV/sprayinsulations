Review insulation work before it is concealed

By Industry Army Marketing (IAM) · September 28, 2026

A review before finishes are installed gives the project team an opportunity to resolve questions while the work is accessible. Arrange that review in the programme rather than treating it as an optional final photograph.

Agree the review points early

Ask the designer, contractor and relevant inspector which stages need review. Put those points into the schedule and identify who gives permission to proceed. The applicable checks depend on the assembly and local requirements.

Compare with the accepted scope

Use the final drawings and approved changes, not an early quote. Record areas that are incomplete or inaccessible and assign follow-up responsibility. Questions about installation quality should go to the qualified reviewer and product requirements rather than being settled by appearance alone.

Keep the closure record

Save the review outcome and dated photographs by location. Identify remaining conditions before closure and obtain the appropriate release. These records help later maintenance teams understand what sits behind the finished surfaces.

Keep with your project brief

  • Scheduled review and responsible people
  • Final scope and outstanding items
  • Documented release for closure
