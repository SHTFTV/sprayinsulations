FOR BUSINESSES

Build a useful relationship.

For website, editorial or industry enquiries, contact build@buildershaus.com with a clear proposal.

Introduce the business

Include your public business name, website, service region and a contact person. Explain what you would like to contribute or discuss, and identify any commercial interest. Do not send client records or confidential documents in an initial enquiry.

Accuracy before promotion

If you propose service information, distinguish what your business currently provides from plans for the future. Support technical statements with current official documents. Credentials, geographic coverage and review claims need evidence before they can be presented as verified. Sending an enquiry does not create a listing or establish an endorsement.

Editorial and commercial boundaries

We want useful information for property owners. Promotional material should be identified as such, and proposed sponsorship must be discussed openly. We do not promise search rankings, paid ranking influence, guaranteed leads or a publication date. Any scope or terms would be agreed separately.

Get in touch

Email build@buildershaus.com. For article proposals, review our guest contribution guidance. For a property assessment, use the project enquiry page.
