The legacy image and its context

The original WeSprayIt service page paired this photograph with its Monoglass section. That association is preserved here. Pipes and suspended services are visible below the coated overhead surface, so photographs for a new enquiry should show both the finish and the obstructions beneath it.

Before planning work

Gather the original specification, product records, drawings and any repair history. Describe the area, access height, occupied use and visible damage. Identify who will review the proposed scope before finishes or services make the work difficult to reach.

Keep repair decisions specific

A matching colour or texture does not establish the installed material or a compatible repair. Ask the assessor to confirm the existing system and document the proposed preparation, replacement area and inspection arrangements.

Questions before an assessment

Where did the Monoglass photo come from?

It was recovered from the WeSprayIt archive, where it appeared with the Monoglass service section.

Was the photograph edited?

A white annotation was removed with AI-assisted cleanup. The original file remains available below.

Does the photo identify the installer?

The available record does not establish the legal installer, location or installation date.

Can the photo establish a fire rating or insulation value?

No. Those depend on the identified product, installed assembly and supporting records.

What should I send with an enquiry?

Send your own overall and close-up photographs, location, access details, the reason for the work and any existing specification.
