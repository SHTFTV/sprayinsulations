CONTRIBUTE SOMETHING USEFUL

Practical expertise, clearly explained.

We welcome proposals that help owners understand insulation decisions, project scope and maintenance records.

Topics that fit

Useful proposals might explain how to prepare an assessment brief, compare a particular part of a proposal, document a renovation or coordinate insulation with other trades. Start with a specific reader question. A narrowly focused article with evidence is more useful than a broad sales pitch.

What to include in your pitch

Send a working title, a short outline, the intended audience and a brief author biography. Include links to relevant published work and the sources you expect to use. Disclose your employer, client relationship, sponsorship or other commercial interest. Explain why the subject belongs on this site.

Standards for publication

Contributions must be original or accompanied by clear permission to publish. Technical claims should link to current primary sources and make their limits clear. Do not invent projects, credentials, reviews or results. Images need documented usage rights and accurate captions; illustrative images must not be presented as customer jobs. We may request revisions and do not guarantee acceptance.

Send the outline first

Email build@buildershaus.com. Do not send confidential customer information. Publication terms, attribution and any commercial arrangement are agreed separately. Acceptance is not a promise of search rankings or a particular link treatment.
