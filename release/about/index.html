ABOUT THIS WEBSITE

Useful information before the quote.

Spray Insulations is an independent planning website for property owners exploring insulation work.

What we help you do

We organize common insulation topics into practical questions about assessments, scope, access and handover. The aim is to make a first enquiry more useful and a written proposal easier to understand. Our pages do not replace a site assessment, a building design or the instructions for a selected product.

Who you are contacting

Lower Mainland project links identify Performance Spray Insulation as the contact. This does not mean the website operates an office in each listed city. Confirm service availability, credentials and the actual scope directly before engaging a contractor. Site, editorial and business enquiries go to build@buildershaus.com.

How we handle technical information

We link to official sources where a guide relies on technical context. We avoid universal installation thicknesses, blanket savings claims and unverified certifications. Requirements depend on the property, assembly, product and location. If you identify a correction, include the page address and supporting source in your message.

What is not claimed

There is no verified national contractor directory or instant estimating service on this release. City pages provide local project-planning context, not proof of a local branch. A contact link is an enquiry route, not a guarantee of booking, eligibility for funding or a particular performance result.
